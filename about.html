Our story

For the small rituals that make a day yours.

ALBORA began with a question: can the things we carry every day feel a little more lasting, useful and beautiful?

In 2022, our founder Nia was tired of replacing bottles that dented, leaked or peeled after a season. So she travelled between her home in Bengaluru and small fabrication workshops in southern India, chasing a more durable answer.

The Field Bottle is the result: a practical object with a quieter footprint, made with responsibly sourced steel and repairable parts. It’s designed to be used hard—and then used again tomorrow.

Made for repeat days

One bottle. A thousand ordinary moments.

“Good design should make room for the day ahead, not demand attention from it.”
